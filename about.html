About HJM International

Built on decades of movement.

HJM International started with a simple observation: businesses lose more time and money to logistics friction than to almost anything else in their supply chain. We set out to close that gap — combining hands-on freight expertise with the kind of technology and communication that keeps every shipment visible, on schedule, and accounted for.
