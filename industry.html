Market Intelligence

Industry Insights.

The logistics industry is evolving in real time — shaped by port congestion, shifting supply chains, fuel volatility, and new technologies.

HJM monitors these trends to stay ahead, optimize operations, and help our partners move smarter across the world.

Key Market Indicators

Data points we track to keep your supply chain ahead of the curve.

📈

US Dry Van Spot Rates

Tracking domestic trucking rate fluctuations to optimize ground freight timing and cost. Rate volatility impacts carrier availability and pricing across all lanes.

🚢

Global Container Freight Index

Monitoring container shipping costs across major trade lanes. Index movements signal shifts in ocean freight capacity, demand, and carrier pricing strategies.

🌐

Global Trade Growth

Tracking trade volume trends across key markets and corridors. Growth patterns inform inventory planning, warehousing needs, and route optimization.

Trends We're Watching

Forces shaping the logistics landscape right now.

Port Congestion & Capacity

Major ports continue to face throughput challenges. We diversify port selection and optimize drayage routes to minimize delays and demurrage charges for our clients.

Supply Chain Nearshoring

Businesses are moving production closer to end markets. We help clients evaluate and execute nearshoring strategies across Mexico, Central America, and the Caribbean.

Fuel & Energy Volatility

Fuel surcharges remain unpredictable. Our multi-modal approach and carrier relationships help stabilize costs even as energy prices fluctuate.

Technology & Automation

From automated warehousing to AI-driven route optimization, technology is transforming logistics. We invest in platforms that give our clients real-time visibility and control.

Regulatory Changes

Tariffs, trade agreements, and sanctions evolve constantly. Our compliance team monitors regulatory shifts across every market we serve.

Sustainability in Logistics

Carbon-conscious shipping is no longer optional. We help clients measure and reduce supply chain emissions through route optimization and modal shifts.
